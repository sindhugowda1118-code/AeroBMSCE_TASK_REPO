A place to learn aerospace engineering by designing, making, testing and flying.

Engineering, beyond the classroom.

AeroBMSCE is the aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club at B.M.S. College of Engineering in Bengaluru.

Members from across the college come together to explore aircraft design, UAV development, drone racing, autonomous systems and the practical work behind flight. The source materials name teams including Yaksha, Blue Impulse, Flying Phantoms and Vectors.

The original club materials describe AeroBMSCE as established in 2012 and students from more than 10 departments as represented. A separate “16+ years” statement conflicts with that history, so this site does not repeat it.

Learn through
the whole flight.

From the first CAD model to fabrication and flight testing, practical teamwork turns engineering theory into real decisions.

A shared vision.

The source describes a future for the club built on design excellence, innovation, competitive success and developing future leaders in aerospace and drones.

The setting listed in the starter is Propel Lab–3 at BMS College of Engineering. Confirm current facilities and contact details before official publication.
