Curiosity, with

AeroBMSCE is the aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, based at Propel Lab–3.

The starter describes a club established in 2012, bringing together students from more than 10 departments. Members explore aircraft design, UAV development, drone racing and autonomous systems through hands-on engineering.
