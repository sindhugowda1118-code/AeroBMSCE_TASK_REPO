Engineering gets
interesting in the air.

AeroBMSCE is the aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club of B.M.S. College of Engineering. Here, ideas move from a first sketch into careful design, fabrication and flight testing.

Meet the club ↗

The work is the classroom

From first principles to first flight.

Members explore fixed-wing aircraft, multirotor UAVs, autonomy and the practical craft behind making things fly. Source materials name teams including Yaksha, Blue Impulse, Flying Phantoms and Vectors.
