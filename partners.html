OUR COLLABORATION PROGRAMME

Engineering grows
through partnership.

Partner support helps students access tools, technical guidance and opportunities to take their work beyond the lab. We welcome conversations about practical collaborations that align with your organisation.

Collaboration tiers

Platinum partner

Title partnership and strategic alignment.

  • Primary logo placement on selected aircraft and UAVs
  • Recognition at key events and in official media
  • Featured placement in reports and competition submissions
  • Direct engagement with team leads

Gold partner

High visibility and competition presence.

  • Logo placement on selected aircraft, uniforms and presentations
  • Shared branding at events and workshops
  • Recognition across competition communications
  • Presence at national and international competitions

Silver partner

Event and media visibility.

  • Logo on team uniforms and project materials
  • Recognition in event communications
  • Social media mentions during competitions
  • Visibility at select AeroBMSCE events

Technical & ecosystem partner

Design, simulation and analysis partnership.

  • Tools acknowledged in design and competition reports
  • Credit in technical presentations and submissions
  • Co-branded hands-on workshops
  • Technical visibility at workshops and student events

A SHARED RETURN

Why partner
with us?

  • Connect with students building real aerospace and UAV systems.
  • Support nationally and internationally ranked competition teams.
  • Engage with practical engineering, fabrication and flight testing.
  • Contribute tools, mentorship and opportunities to an active student community.

Research and development areas

  • Indigenous flight-controller and ESC development
  • GPS-denied navigation and sensor-led autonomy
  • Multi-mission fixed-wing aircraft and modular payload integration
  • Morphing wingtip mechanisms and directional stability research

Project details and external collaboration are discussed with the college and team before any commitment.
